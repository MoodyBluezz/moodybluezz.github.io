DailyQuests: RPG Habits

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026 · Effective: 1 October 2026

In short. Your quests, notes, name and progress stay on your device. There are no accounts and no ads. If you choose to allow it, we receive anonymous usage statistics and crash reports — never quest titles, never names, never anything that identifies you. You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Privacy.

1. Who we are

DailyQuests: RPG Habits (the "App") is developed and published by Moozy Games ("we"). Privacy questions: moozygames@proton.me.

2. What stays on your device

Quests, notes, progress, companions, settings and your profile photo (if you chose one) are kept in the App's local database on your device and are never sent anywhere. System backup of the App's data is turned off. Clear the App's data or uninstall it to delete everything.

3. What we receive — only with your consent

Nothing in this section is collected when you install the App. On the day after you first set it up, we ask once: "Help make DailyQuests better?". Until you answer "Yes", and if you answer "No", nothing is sent.

Anonymous statistics — events with technical names, values from fixed lists, and numbers rounded into ranges ("2–3 quests done yesterday", never the quests themselves):

  • the first-run tutorial: which steps were completed or skipped, how long it took, which companion was chosen, the type and difficulty of the first quest;
  • activity: the first launch of the day and how many quests were done the day before, streaks, levels, trophies, the morning and evening rituals;
  • the companion: care, bond, growth stages, expeditions;
  • the Valley, the Hoard and Embers: where they were opened from, which item Embers were spent on and in which price range, Pro gifts;
  • notifications: which kind of notification you opened;
  • purchases: which purchase screen was shown, which plan or item was chosen, how the purchase ended — no amounts, order numbers or payment details;
  • settings and errors: whether "less motion" is on, the kind of an App error;
  • what Firebase Analytics adds on its own: device model, Android version, language, country (from the IP address, no precise location), App version, session times, a random App-instance id.

Crash reports — technical details of a crash: stack trace, device model, Android version, App version, build number and language, the device's state at the time (such as free memory), a random installation id.

4. What is always sent

To receive the App's settings (for example, whether an item or a seasonal event is on), the App contacts Firebase Remote Config at most once every 12 hours. This sends the Firebase installation id (a random value not linked to you) and the technical details needed to serve the settings: App and Android version, device language and time zone. It carries nothing about how you use the App. Offline, the App runs on its built-in settings.

5. What we never collect

Quest titles or descriptions, notes, your name or your companion's name, any text you type; e-mail, phone, contacts, photos; precise location; the advertising id (the App does not even request it) or the Android id. The App shows no ads and shares nothing with ad networks.

6. Who processes the data

Statistics, crash reports and settings are processed by Google (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config) as our processor — on our behalf, under the Firebase data processing terms. More: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy. We do not sell data or share it with anyone else.

7. Retention

Statistics are kept for at most 2 months, crash reports for at most 90 days, then deleted automatically.

8. Purchases

The subscription and items are sold and billed by Google Play. We never see your Google account, card number or other payment details — only Google receives them, under its own privacy policy. The App keeps on your device only which purchases are active. Checking for App updates also goes through Google Play.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Settings → Privacy — "Anonymous statistics" and "Crash reports" separately. Turning one off stops sending at once and deletes whatever is queued on the device.
  • We do not know who you are, so we cannot look up "your" records by name or e-mail. Statistics are anonymous and expire after 2 months. For questions or requests, write to us — we will answer.

10. Children

The App is meant for a general audience and collects nothing that identifies anyone, including children. Statistics and crash reports are sent only after explicit consent.

11. Changes

If we change how data is handled (for example, optional accounts or sync), we will update this page and the date above, and ask for consent again in the App for any new kind of data.
