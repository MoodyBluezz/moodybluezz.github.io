Ridge Oasis: Cozy City Builder

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026 · Effective: 27 October 2026

In short. Ridge Oasis is a single-player game. Your cities, settings and photos stay on your device. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no purchases, and the App never connects to the internet. The only thing that can leave your device is a city file you choose to share yourself.

1. Who we are

Ridge Oasis: Cozy City Builder (the "App") is developed and published by Moozy Games ("we"). Privacy questions: moozygames@proton.me.

2. What stays on your device

Your cities (saves and the autosave), your settings (graphics preset, language, sound) and the photos you take in the App's photo mode are kept in the App's local storage on your device. Photos are saved on the device only. None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. Clear the App's data or uninstall it to delete everything the App stored on the device.

3. What we never collect

The App makes no network requests at all. It has no accounts or sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no purchases, no push notifications and no third-party SDKs. We receive nothing: not your name, e-mail, contacts or location, no device identifiers, and nothing about how you play. On Android the App's package lists the INTERNET permission only because the game engine adds it by default; the App never uses it. Nothing leaves your device.

4. City files you share

You can export a city as a file and share it yourself by any means you like, such as a messenger or cloud storage. Only you decide whether and with whom to share it; the App never uploads it anywhere. A city file contains the city layout: buildings, roads, furniture and the generated names of its residents. It contains nothing about you or your device. Opening a city file someone sent you is read-only ("visiting"): you can look around, the file is not changed and nothing is sent back.

5. Backups

On Android the App's data may be included in your device's system backup (Google backup under your Google account, according to Google's policy) if you have backup turned on. We have no access to those backups. Clearing the App's data or uninstalling it deletes everything on the device.

6. Children

The App is meant for a general audience and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

If a future version adds anything that handles data differently (for example online features, purchases or statistics), we will update this page and the date above, and the App will ask for your consent for any new kind of data before sending it.

8. Contact
